# GhostTrace AI 👻 — 60–90 Second Demo Video Script

This script provides an exact step-by-step guide for recording a concise, polished 60–90 second demonstration video for Hacktoberfest and social showcases.

---

## 🎬 Pre-Recording Checklist

1. Start Ollama with `ollama serve` (or ensure `qwen2.5:3b` is pulled).
2. Launch the application:
   ```powershell
   streamlit run app.py
   ```
3. Set your browser to dark mode and maximize the window (`1080p` or `1440p` recommended).
4. Collapse any unnecessary browser toolbars.

---

## ⏱️ Video Walkthrough Breakdown (75–90 Seconds)

### Segment 1: Introduction & Theme Hook (0:00 – 0:15)
- **Visual:** Show the GhostTrace AI header banner and dashboard with glowing metrics (*Events Analyzed*, *Flagged Anomalies*, *Peak Mystery Score*, *Paranormal Threat Index*).
- **Voiceover / Action:**
  > "Welcome to GhostTrace AI, a spooky local AI storytelling terminal built for the Hacktoberfest 'Ghost in the Machine' challenge. Ever wondered what your computer does when the screen goes black at 3:00 AM? GhostTrace analyzes simulated system logs and turns anomalous events into eerie detective ghost stories—running 100% locally with open-source AI."

---

### Segment 2: Case Selection & Transparent Mystery Detector (0:15 – 0:35)
- **Visual:**
  1. In the sidebar, select **Case 001: The Midnight Process**.
  2. Click on the first tab: **🔍 Anomaly Scanner & Mystery Report**.
  3. Scroll down through the cards showing the witching hour event at `03:33:07` and the `0xDEADBEEF` pointer.
  4. Expand the **Inspect Raw Telemetry** dropdown for one event.
- **Voiceover / Action:**
  > "We'll open Case 001: The Midnight Process. GhostTrace uses a transparent, deterministic rule engine that assigns mystery scores from 0 to 100. Notice how this event at 3:33 AM is flagged with a 100/100 score because it triggered during the witching hour and called a spontaneous daemon process without a parent PID. Every single flag is fully explainable."

---

### Segment 3: Paranormal Timeline Inspection (0:35 – 0:50)
- **Visual:**
  1. Click on the second tab: **⏳ Paranormal Investigation Timeline**.
  2. Uncheck 'Low' or filter by 'Process' to show the chronological sequence.
  3. Expand a card to show the time flow.
- **Voiceover / Action:**
  > "The interactive timeline allows investigators to trace the supernatural chronology, filtering by severity and event types to pinpoint exactly when the digital ghost began manifesting."

---

### Segment 4: Local AI Story Generation with Qwen2.5 3B (0:50 – 1:15)
- **Visual:**
  1. Click on the third tab: **📜 Ghost Story Terminal**.
  2. Select the story style: **Haunted Computer** or **Digital Ghost Mystery**.
  3. Click **🕯️ Generate Story**.
  4. Show the dossier container populating with the atmospheric story incorporating timestamps (`03:33:07`) and hex memory addresses.
  5. Point out the badge: `⚙️ Engine: Ollama (qwen2.5:3b)`.
  6. Click the **💾 Save Story (.txt)** button to show download functionality.
- **Voiceover / Action:**
  > "Now for the magic: our Local AI Story Engine takes the flagged evidence and prompts Qwen2.5 3B running locally via Ollama. It weaves the exact timestamps and memory addresses into an atmospheric thriller. Zero cloud APIs, zero costs, and 100% privacy."

---

### Segment 5: Offline Fallback & Wrap-Up (1:15 – 1:30)
- **Visual:**
  1. Toggle **Force Offline Fallback Mode** in the sidebar.
  2. Click **🔄 Regenerate** to show immediate procedural story generation.
  3. Briefly flip to Tab 4: **🛡️ Safety, Privacy & Open AI** showing the architecture diagram and privacy disclaimer.
- **Voiceover / Action:**
  > "Even if Ollama is offline, GhostTrace's built-in procedural narrative engine guarantees seamless offline storytelling. Check out the project on GitHub, test the 5 built-in cases, and happy Hacktoberfest!"

---

## 💡 Recording Tips
- **Pacing:** Speak at a calm, engaging pace with a slightly mysterious tone.
- **Audio:** Use a clean microphone with noise suppression enabled.
- **Mouse Movement:** Move the cursor smoothly without erratic wandering.
